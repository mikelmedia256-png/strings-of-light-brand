I

Overture

Adagio · the idea in one breath

Before anyone hears her, they see her: one figure, a violin, and the light coming in behind.

Strings of Light is one woman and one violin. There is no ensemble or agency behind her, and no stock playlist. Couples are buying a person: a trained musician who chooses every piece with them and plays it live, close enough to see.

So the mark is Claire herself, standing in the light her name promises.

The emblem

Claire's own silhouette, traced from a photograph of her playing: the bun, the profile, the raised bow. Not a stock violinist.

She stands in an arch, the shape from her first graphics and the door of every chapel she plays in. Behind her the sky moves from night violet to gold, sampled from the photographs she chose as her inspiration: light breaking through dark cloud, violet night skies, gold at dusk.

The light

Low, warm, breaking through darkness. Every piece has one source of it.

The figure

Claire, always playing. The silhouette comes from this photograph.

The arch

A window, a chapel door. The frame that lets the light in.

The line

A moment, held.

Three words that work on a business card, at the end of a reel and under a bride's first look. Always with its full stop.

V

Tone colour

Dolce · six colours sampled from the mood board

Each colour was sampled from Claire's mood board: night skies, purple cloud, amber light at a cloud's edge, gold dusk, backlit grass and light breaking through a storm.

The sky

The five colours together, top to bottom, are light breaking through darkness. It is the brand's one flourish.

  • Inside the emblem, always.
  • One hero moment per piece: a cover, a website band, a reel's last frame.
  • Always vertical, night at the top and gold at the bottom, the way the sky sits.
  • Never behind body text.

In use: dawn and night carry almost everything; the sky colours are light, so they are used the way light is, in small amounts. Cloud (#E6E0EC) is a lilac mist for second grounds.

Night on dawnBody text, the signature. 15.2:1
Dawn on nightReversed text. 15.2:1
Halo on nightLabels, buttons, accents. 9.4:1

Ember and halo are never text on dawn (2.6:1 and less).

VII

Light

Sostenuto · how Claire is photographed

One light

Light from one side or from behind: a window, low sun, a single spot. Let the rest fall into shadow.

Shoot the silhouette

At golden hour, put the sun behind her. Backlit frames are the emblem made real, and the next shoot should be built around them.

Playing, not posing

She is mid-phrase in the best pictures. No holding the violin like a prop.

Warm or black & white

Grade warm toward halo, never cool or teal. Black and white for the stage and for memorials.

VIII

Words

Con amore · how Strings of Light speaks

Write the way Claire talks to a nervous bride on the phone: warmly, briefly, and with complete confidence that the day will be beautiful.

Short sentences. One image, not three. Speak to "you". Claire is a violinist, never a vocalist; she works across the Southeast, not one city; the repertoire is contemporary, sacred and classical, in that order. The only public price is ceremony music from $450.

Write

Is your date still open? Tell me about your day.

Not

Submit an inquiry to check vendor availability for your event!!

Write

The walk down the aisle takes about ninety seconds. Let's choose the music for it together.

Not

We offer a wide range of premium musical packages for every occasion.

Write

Played for Sara and Tom on Saturday. A moment, held.

Not

Another magical day!! ✨🎻💕 #weddingmusic #violinist #blessed

  • Ceremony, prelude, processional
  • Live, by hand, together
  • Golden hour, candlelight, first look

Words we leave out

  • Magical, stunning, epic
  • Packages, vendor, booking solutions
  • Vocalist, band, DJ
  • Strings For Light
